01 · Start with one page

Choose a real task. Give it a clear address, readable content, and a useful next action.

02 · Share a working version

Let someone open the page directly. Check its links on a small screen and with JavaScript disabled.

03 · Improve one thing at a time

Keep shared pieces together. Make each change easy to explain and verify.
