# Bundled assets

The starter serves its stylesheet locally and requires no browser CDN.

| Dependency | Version used for this build | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tailwind CSS CLI | 4.2.2 | [MIT](static/vendor/tailwindcss-LICENSE.txt) |
| daisyUI | 5.5.19 | [MIT](static/vendor/daisyui-LICENSE.txt) |

`resources/css/site.css` and the templates are the stylesheet's source.
`static/css/site.css` is its generated output.

The two JPEG originals are copies of `item-01.jpg` and `item-02.jpg` from the
repository's `catalog-store/resources/images/products` collection. Those images
were generated for the catalog example; no external image request is made.
